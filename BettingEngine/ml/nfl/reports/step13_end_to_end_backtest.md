# NFL Step 13 — end-to-end backtest

This is the consolidated EPL-style NFL backtest: one row per game, three core
markets, model forecasts, opening and closing lines, results and threshold
summaries. It combines 1,599 walk-forward development games (2019–2024) with
the untouched 272-game sealed 2025 vault.

The report distinguishes predictive accuracy from betting evidence. Historical
opening coverage is incomplete and bookmaker prices were not archived, so every
ROI figure is explicitly synthetic -110 arithmetic. Odds never entered the
model forecast.

The output is suitable for paper pricing immediately. It is not evidence that
NFL staking should begin. Genuine promotion still requires the prospective
ledger, audited obtainable openers and prices, positive CLV and the frozen gates.
