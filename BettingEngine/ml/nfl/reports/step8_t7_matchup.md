# NFL Step 8I — T7 scheme/matchup audit

## Decision

Reject T7 as a separate predictive tier. Preserve the interactions only as
research diagnostics; they cannot change a price or create a bet.

Across 1,599 walk-forward games from 2019–2024, T7 improved margin MAE by only
0.005 points, worsened RMSE, and improved only three of six seasons. It moved
farther from the closing spread (2.902 versus 2.844 points). For totals it made
MAE 0.023 points worse and improved only one season. No 2025 vault games were
used.

This means the existing T1 rolling offence and defence state already captures
almost all useful information in these simple matchup products. Adding a label
called “matchup” does not create a separate edge.
