# NFL Step 8K — T9 matrix confluence

## Decision

Freeze the discovered spread rule for a 2026 prospective shadow. It remains a
WATCH signal with no bet or stake authorised.

T9 counts signal families, not model rows. Structural team strength, independent
ML and the incremental T2/T3 personnel effect must point in the same direction.
Two team-strength variants still count as only one family. A conflict forces an
abstain, and stale inputs are excluded.

Across 2020–2024, the retrospective spread rule selected 124 games. Among 103
where the line moved, it anticipated the closing direction 78.6% of the time and
recorded mean CLV of +1.79 points. Synthetic opener grading was 67 wins, 55
losses and two pushes: 54.9% excluding pushes. Directional CLV was positive in
every season, but the 2022 game-result record was only 12–14.

The totals discovery also showed positive CLV, but it uses observed historical
weather rather than archived forecasts. It is an oracle and cannot qualify as a
live betting rule.

## Why this is not proof

The two-point threshold was discovered after looking at historical results,
historical true-opener provenance is incomplete, exact prices are unavailable,
and personnel/weather timestamps are not fully prospective. The rule must now
remain unchanged while 2026 quotes, line moves, personnel and forecasts are
captured. Success means positive CLV across a meaningful frozen sample—not
retuning after individual losses.
