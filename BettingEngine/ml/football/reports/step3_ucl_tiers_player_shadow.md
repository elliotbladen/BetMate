# Step 3 — UCL tiers and player shadow

The UCL stack now mirrors EPL/EFL T0–T3 and T6–T8, with two UCL-specific
shadows: T4 league-phase incentives and T5 knockout aggregate state. T2 is the
shared residual player/personnel shadow. Only T0 data health and T1 club
strength may influence the paper price initially; all other tiers remain
diagnostic until their own walk-forward gates pass. T2 explicitly includes
injury status, expected minutes, player impact, replacement level and
suspension. A star-player absence can therefore move the shadow price, but the
goal-rate correction is capped at 0.12 until historical injury data validates a
larger effect.
