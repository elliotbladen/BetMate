# UCL knockout-state validation

The modern archive contains, for each of 2024/25 and 2025/26:

- 16 knockout-playoff matches (the 9–24 league-phase clubs)
- 29 knockout-phase matches (round of 16 through final)
- 45 knockout matches in total

The records are retained as individual legs/matches. They do not yet contain a
complete tie-ID and first-leg/second-leg pairing for every historical tie, so
they are not being used to claim qualification probabilities. The next build
will add tie pairing, aggregate state, extra-time/penalty outcomes and a
walk-forward progression simulation.
