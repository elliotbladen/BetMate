# UCL knockout progression targets

The paired-tie archive is now the historical target set for progression
backtesting. Each modern season contains:

- 8 two-legged playoff ties (16 playoff matches before tie-level pairing)
- 8 Round-of-16 ties
- 4 quarterfinal ties
- 2 semifinal ties
- 1 single-match final

At tie level, aggregate winners are recorded for every two-leg pairing. The
final is retained as a single match because it has no second leg. These labels
are now ready for a model simulation that carries first-leg aggregate score,
home/away reversal, extra time and penalties. No progression accuracy or ROI is
claimed until those probabilities are generated from pre-match forecasts.
