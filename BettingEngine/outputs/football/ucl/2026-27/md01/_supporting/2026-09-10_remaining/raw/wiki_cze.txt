The '''2026–27 Czech First League''', known as the '''[[Czech First League|Chance Liga]]''' for sponsorship reasons, will be the 34th season of the [[Czech Republic]]'s top-tier [[association football|football]] for professional clubs since its establishment, in 1993. [[SK Slavia Prague|Slavia Prague]] are the reigning champions. The season is scheduled to start in July 2026. The first half of the season will have 18 rounds, finishing in December, and the other half will commence on 30 January 2027. The season is expected to end on 29 May 2027 with two extra play-out fixtures on 3–6 June 2027.<ref>{{cite web |title=Ligové grémium schválilo termínovou listinu pro sezonu 2026/27. Platby solidarity přinesou klubům více peněz|url=https://www.lfafotbal.cz/clanek/912-ligove-gremium-schvalilo-terminovou-listinu-pro-sezonu-2026-27-platby-solidarity-prinesou-klubum-vice-penez|publisher=League Football Association|language=cs|date=2025-12-03}}</ref><ref name=idnes>{{cite web |last=Novák|first=Miloslav|title=Příští sezona fotbalové ligy začne šest dnů po finále MS, bude opět s nadstavbou|url=https://www.idnes.cz/fotbal/prvni-liga/chance-liga-2026-2027-prvni-liga-terminova-listina-terminovka.A251203_131317_fotbal_min|work=[[iDNES.cz]]|language=cs|date=2025-12-03}}</ref>

==Format==
The general season format is unchanged from last season. The 16 clubs will play each other home and away, until the league is split up in championship, play-off and relegation groups. The lowest-ranked team will be relegated directly to the second league, and the two teams positioned 14th and 15th will play a play-out with two teams from the second league positioned 2nd and 3rd in a home and away format. [[Video assistant referee|VAR]] will be featured at all matches played. There are no changes in the rules. The only adjustment compared to last season is the match schedule, which has been adapted to the [[2026 FIFA World Cup]], and therefore only 18 rounds are planned for the autumn part of the league instead of the usual 19.<ref name=idnes/>

===Promotion and relegation (pre-season)===
A total of sixteen teams contest the league, including thirteen sides from the 2025–26 season, the winner of last season's second league, and two winners of the play-out, determined on 30–31 May 2026.

;Team promoted to Czech First League
After being relegated in the [[2022–23 Czech First League|2022–23]] season, [[FC Zbrojovka Brno]] returns to Czech First League as the champion of the [[2025–26 Czech National Football League]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Zbrojovka se vrací do první ligy! Po remíze Artisu s Táborskem má o postupu jasno|url=https://www.idnes.cz/fotbal/domaci-souteze/zbrojovka-se-vraci-do-prvni-ligy-po-remize-artisu-s-taborskem-ma-o-postupu-jasno.A260420_190104_fot_dsouteze_tit|work=[[iDNES.cz]]|language=cs|date=2026-04-20|agency=[[Czech News Agency]]}}</ref>

;Teams relegated from Czech First League
The lowest positioned team from the [[2025–26 Czech First League|last season]], [[FK Dukla Prague]], was relegated to the Czech National Football League.

;Karviná match-fixing
On 15 June 2026, [[MFK Karviná]] was administratively relegated to the second-tier FNL by the Ethics Committee of the [[Football Association of the Czech Republic|FAČR]] due to alleged [[2026 Czech football match-fixing scandal|match-fixing]].<ref>{{cite news |last=Smrkovský|first=Lukáš|url=https://ct24.ceskatelevize.cz/clanek/domaci/eticka-komise-facr-vyloucila-karvinou-z-prvni-ligy-kvuli-ovlivnovani-zapasu-374612|title=Etická komise FAČR vyloučila Karvinou z první ligy kvůli ovlivňování zápasů|work=[[ČT24]]|date=15 June 2026|access-date=26 June 2026|publisher=[[Czech Television]]|language=cs|agency=[[Czech News Agency]]}}</ref> The club appealed the decision and the fixtures of the 2026–27 season were drawn with Karviná as a member of the league. On 28 June 2026, the club decided to withdraw its appeal and leave the Czech First League. Because the 2025–26 season was officially concluded on 22 June, the Karviná's place in the league was preferentially offered to the Czech National Football League's last season best teams. After [[FC Silon Táborsko]] rejected the offer, Karviná was replaced by [[SK Artis Brno]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Artis Brno přijal nabídku od LFA a v příští sezoně bude hrát první ligu|url=https://www.seznamzpravy.cz/clanek/sport-fotbal-artis-brno-prijal-nabidku-od-lfa-a-v-pristi-sezone-bude-hrat-prvni-ligu-310003|work=Seznam Zprávy|language=cs|date=4 July 2026|access-date=4 July 2026}}</ref>
