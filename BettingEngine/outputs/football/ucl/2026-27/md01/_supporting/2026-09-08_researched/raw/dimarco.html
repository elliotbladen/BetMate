Inter Without Dimarco Against Real Madrid: Wing-back Stays in Milan with Knee Problem

Serie A
Calcio

Prudence has won out at the Nerazzurri camp: following a slight sprain in the match against Napoli, coach Chivu decided to rest the player from the away trip.

Filippo Conticello
7 september (modifica alle 14:25) - MILANO
Italian version
Scegli Gazzetta come fonte preferita su Google

Federico Dimarco will not travel with Inter to Madrid. Caution prevailed after the slight sprain to his left knee sustained on Saturday against Napoli. The wing-back had felt pain in the first half but returned after the interval with a noticeable bandage. He even provided Lautaro with the assist for the 2-1 goal before succumbing to the discomfort and asking to be substituted in the 62nd minute. 

VIDEO: Inter, Chivu scherza in allenamento: "Non è mano! Come Lobotka..."

—  

Tests have ruled out any serious problems, but with the Bernabéu clash so close, Inter decided not to take any risks. Dimarco will stay in Milan to fully recover, while the left flank against Real will be covered by Carlos Augusto, who also provided an assist against Napoli.
