The '''2026–27 Azerbaijan Premier League''' will be the 35th season of the [[Azerbaijan Premier League]], the highest tier football league of Azerbaijan. The season will start on 14 August 2026 and end on 24 May 2027.<ref>{{cite web |title=Premyer Liqada növbəti mövsüm bu tarixdə başlayacaq |url=https://sportfm.az/78119-premyer-liqada-novbeti-movsum-bu-tarixde-bashlayacaq.html |website=sportfm.az |publisher=sportfm.az |access-date=23 May 2026 |language=Azerbaijani}}</ref>

[[Sabah FK (Azerbaijan)|Sabah]] will enter the season as the defending champions.

== Teams ==
Twelve teams will compete in the league – the top eleven teams from the previous season and one team promoted from the [[2025–26 Azerbaijan First League|Azerbaijan First League]].
