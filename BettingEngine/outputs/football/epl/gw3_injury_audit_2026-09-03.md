# EPL Week 3 first-team availability audit

Date: 3 September 2026

Primary round tracker: Squawka/Premier Injuries compilation updated 2 September 2026, cross-referenced where possible against club reporting. Liverpool's long-term absences were independently confirmed by Liverpool FC's official availability page.

## T5 operating rule

- Confirmed important first-team absences are included in the normal production price.
- Players listed as doubts are not included until confirmed absent. They remain late-test scenarios.
- Long lists were filtered for material senior first-team roles; academy/depth absences were not automatically counted.
- Position codes, not player quality, drive current T5. This is a bounded approximation and can overstate clusters of long-term absences already partly reflected in team results.
