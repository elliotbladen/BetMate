## Data Limitations

1. **ELO is R23-vintage:** features_afl.csv ends at R23 (Aug 16). R24 ELO updates are estimated (K~30 adjustment). A proper game_log rebuild would give exact ELOs.
2. **ML margin model overcooks:** Raw margin predictions (-29, -35) are 5-6x market lines. Known issue. H2H legacy model is well-calibrated and should be trusted for H2H direction.
3. **EMA features are R23 + estimated R24 update:** Proper EMA computation requires running game_log.py with R24 results loaded. Manual estimates introduce ~2-3pt uncertainty.
4. **T2 style matchup estimated:** No fresh Footywire snapshot for wildcard round. Used directional assessment from season-long profiles.
5. **Finals context:** Model trained primarily on H&A games. `is_final=1` flag is set but limited finals games in training data may reduce reliability.
