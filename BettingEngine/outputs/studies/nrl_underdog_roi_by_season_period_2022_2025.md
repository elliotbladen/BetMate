# NRL closing-price underdog ROI by season period (2022–2025)

Regular season only. One flat $1 bet on the longer-priced H2H team in every game. Closing price is used where available; the historical workbook's opening-price fallback is used only where its close is absent. Equal-priced teams are excluded.

Periods are fixed before calculation: early R1–10, Origin R11–20, late R21 through the end of the regular season. Finals are excluded.

## Interpretation

The early season was the only profitable pooled period and was profitable in all four individual seasons. Origin and late-season underdogs lost money in the pooled sample.

The bootstrap interval is wide because long-priced winners create volatile returns. The result is descriptive and is not, by itself, proof of a future betting edge. A prospective rule should be frozen before testing 2026.

Data checks: 788 qualifying regular-season games; 16 equal-price games excluded.
