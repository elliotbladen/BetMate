# Handover — Rosehill 29 August timing gap and required rerating

Date: 31 August 2026

## Finding

The Rosehill meeting from Saturday 29 August 2026 is present in the racing
database from the structured NSW-results fallback, but its official race and
runner clocks were not present in the clean V2 layer. Those clocks were marked
`quarantined` because the source values were null, not because an observed
clock failed a plausibility test.

Guest House's stored `form-first-v2.0` achieved-run rating of 98.4626 for Race
8 was therefore a collateral/class-standard assessment only. It used a
collateral anchor of 96.8282 at 80% and the Group 3 class standard of 105 at
20%. It must not be described as clock-confirmed. The imported 102 is the
horse's official pre-race handicap rating and was not generated by BetMate.

## Agreed action

Acquire and archive the authorised Racing NSW timing/sectional report for the
whole 29 August Rosehill meeting, import its observed official race and runner
times, rebuild the clean V2 meeting and downstream ratings, and then report the
revised figures with their evidence status. Do not invent or manually infer
missing times. If the authorised report is unavailable, retain the current
ratings as explicitly provisional collateral-only figures and flag the source
gap to the user.

## Acquisition result

The unqualified Racing NSW `2908ROSE` URL returned a 20-page report dated **29
August 2020**, not 2026. The importer now rejects that recycled response. The
year-qualified ATC Swiss Timing archive supplied the correct 2026 report, and
all ten races were reconciled by runner number plus horse identity.

After rebuilding V2 through 31 August, Guest House has a valid 62.41-second
individual clock and complete 200m sectionals. Its `form-first-v2.0` point
rating remains 98.4626 because that production model does not use time to alter
the rating; the valid clock raises the run's evidence confidence to 0.88. This
distinction is important: the timing recovery verifies and enriches the run,
but the existing 98.46 is still a form/collateral rating, not a speed figure.

## Communication rule

Whenever a requested rating lacks a valid clock, say so in the first answer.
Always distinguish BetMate achieved-run rating, BetMate sustainable Ability,
and the external official handicap rating.
