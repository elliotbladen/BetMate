# Sydney three-year sectional coverage audit

Audit window: 1 September 2023 through 30 August 2026  
Tracks: Randwick and Rosehill  
Population: finished runners in the current V2 clean result layer

## Result

- 136 meetings and 14,849 finished runner-performances audited.
- 14,469 runs have sectionals including a finish marker (97.4%).
- 13 runs have partial sectionals but no finish marker (0.1%).
- 367 runs have no sectional data (2.5%). These include horses the ATC report
  says it could not track and result/card anomalies; they are not all
  recoverable sectional observations.
- 341 unique horses are affected by at least one missing run.
- No meeting is completely missing sectionals. Seventy-five meetings contain
  at least one runner without tracking data.
- 1,322 Sydney races now have a valid official clock and 14,366 runner-runs
  have a valid individual finish clock in the rebuilt V2 clean layer.

The old unqualified Racing NSW URLs for 22 and 29 August 2026 returned recycled
2020 reports. The importer now rejects those by meeting date and uses ATC's
year-qualified archive; both 2026 meetings have been recovered correctly.

## Evidence limitation

All 136 meeting reports are now retained locally from ATC's year-qualified
archive and have passed embedded-date validation. The importer reconciles both
runner number and normalized horse identity, and resolves the small set of
cards whose Racing.com race numbering was displaced by matching whole fields.

Two scheduled Randwick cards (22 June 2024 and 9 August 2025) have no ATC timing
report because racing was postponed/abandoned before completion. They are not
evidence gaps for completed races and should ultimately be excluded from the
source result population rather than supplied with invented sectionals.

The full affected-horse list and every missing runner-performance are in the
companion CSV files generated by `audit_sydney_sectionals.py`.

## Recovery-source status

Ordinary Punting Form access does not provide sectional API permission. Its
published sectional endpoint belongs to the separately negotiated
Modeller/commercial product, and the project has no such credential. Do not
treat Punting Form as an available backfill source.

The recovery source is the Australian Turf Club's public Swiss Timing archive.
It supplies the runner-level 200m intervals, cumulative clocks and in-running
positions used here. Where a report ends its cumulative table at the 200m but
publishes Last 600m, the importer derives the finish clock as cumulative-at-600
plus Last 600m and retains the component evidence.
