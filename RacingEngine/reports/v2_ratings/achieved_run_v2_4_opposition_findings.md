Date: 28 August 2026

Decision: **not promoted; revise or reject**

## Candidate

The candidate builds a strictly pre-race opposition anchor for the first four
finishers. It combines each principal's official rating with the median of up
to three prior accepted V2 runs. History depth raises reliability; dispersion
reduces it. The race-level opposition anchor is the median of margin- and
weight-aligned principal anchors.

The revision coefficient was selected only on 43 pre-2025 dominant-winner
examples with an observable 90-day future peak. Natural Fling, Sheza Alibi and
Gringotts were excluded. The fixed 0.00--1.00 grid selected **0.20**: training
MAE improved from 8.0613 at zero to 7.9490, while a full revision worsened it
to 8.8914.

## Frozen audits

- Natural Fling: **89.68**, down from V2.3's 91.73 and outside 100--110.
- Sheza Alibi: **116.24**; Gringotts: **112.37**. WFA semantics pass and

- Frozen breakout future-peak MAE passes both baselines at all three horizons:
  90 days 8.106 versus class-only 9.108 and official 12.307; 180 days 8.913
  versus 8.972 and 14.224; 365 days 9.339 versus 9.428 and 17.783.

## Natural Fling opposition audit

Her point-in-time opposition anchor is only 77.53. Scintillation supplies the
strongest principal line (90.72 after aligning the four-length margin), but the
other three principal anchors are 71.95, 78.46 and 76.60. The fitted 20%
revision therefore subtracts 2.05 points. There is not yet meaningful later
collateral in the database to overturn that pre-race assessment.

## Decision

The family contains useful broad collateral information but does not explain
Natural Fling's achieved performance. Do not promote it or rerun Horse Ability.
Preserve it as a shadow opposition component. The unresolved evidence is the
winner's own dominance/achievement rather than the demonstrated pre-race
quality of this field; subsequent collateral can be appended prospectively.
