# Achieved Run V2.5 dominance findings

Date: 28 August 2026  
Version: `achieved-run-v2.5-dominance-pit-shadow`  
Decision: **rejected at coefficient zero**

## Candidate and fit

Winning margins were ranked only against strictly earlier races in the same
class family and distance band. No dominance authority is assigned below the
prior 75th percentile; authority rises to one at the top of the distribution.
The candidate then tested whether that independent rarity signal should restore
more of the full V2.1 achieved-run update.

The fixed coefficient grid was selected on 158 pre-2025 winners with observable
90-day future peaks. Natural Fling, Ninja, Sheza Alibi and Gringotts were
excluded. The selected coefficient was **0.00**: MAE was 7.4709 at zero and
worsened monotonically to 7.8474 at one. No rating changed from V2.4.

## Named audit

- Natural Fling's four-length Group 3 sprint win ranks at the **98.58th
  percentile** among 141 strictly prior comparable races. Dominance reliability
  is 0.943, but the fitted coefficient zero leaves her at **89.68**.
- Ninja's 1.2-length Group 2 sprint win ranks at the **81.25th percentile**
  among 80 prior comparable races. Reliability is 0.25 and the figure remains
  **112.94**.
- Sheza Alibi remains above Gringotts, 116.24 to 112.37.

## Interpretation

The rarity evidence behaves plausibly, but the fitting target is wrong for an
achieved-run component. A next-90-day peak measures repeatability and future
ability; it penalises exceptional one-day performances through mean reversion.
That is appropriate for Horse Ability shrinkage, not for deciding what a horse
achieved in the completed race.

Do not override the zero coefficient or promote this family. The next build
must register separate targets for (1) achieved run merit and (2) sustainable
or projected ability. Dominance can inform the former; recurrence, uncertainty
and subsequent form govern the latter.
